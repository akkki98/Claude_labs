# Lab 3: Claude for Business Productivity

## Lab Overview

In this hands-on lab, you will use Claude to improve everyday business productivity tasks such as writing professional content, drafting emails, generating business reports, and performing business analysis.

Instead of writing everything manually, you will learn how to collaborate with Claude using effective prompts and iterative conversations to produce high-quality business documents.

---

## Lab Objectives

By the end of this lab, you will be able to:

- Create business content using Claude
- Draft professional emails for different scenarios
- Generate structured reports from raw information
- Perform business analysis using Claude
- Improve responses through follow-up prompts

---

## Estimated Duration

**60 Minutes**

| Section | Duration |
|----------|-----------|
| Content Creation | 15 mins |
| Email Drafting | 15 mins |
| Report Generation | 15 mins |
| Business Analysis | 15 mins |

- Claude account
- Web browser
- Basic understanding of business communication

# Business Scenario

You work as a Business Operations Manager for **Contoso Electronics**, a company that manufactures consumer electronic products.

Your daily responsibilities include:

- Preparing marketing content
- Responding to customers
- Creating weekly reports
- Analyzing sales trends
- Providing recommendations to management

Throughout this lab, you will use Claude as your AI business assistant.

---

# Exercise 1 – Content Creation

## Scenario

The marketing department is launching a new AI-powered smart speaker called **Contoso SmartHome Hub**.

You need promotional content for different platforms.

---

## Task 1.1 – Generate Product Description

### Prompt

```
You are a professional marketing copywriter.

Create a product description for the Contoso SmartHome Hub.

Features:
- AI Voice Assistant
- Smart Home Automation
- Premium Sound
- Energy Efficient
- Works with Android and iOS

Target audience:
Working professionals and families.

Tone:
Professional, engaging, and easy to understand.

Limit to 200 words.
```

---

### Expected Outcome

Claude generates:

- Product overview
- Key features
- Benefits
- Call to action

---

## Task 1.2 – Create LinkedIn Post

Prompt:

```
Create a professional LinkedIn announcement introducing our new AI-powered SmartHome Hub.

Requirements:

- 150 words
- Professional tone
- Include relevant hashtags
- End with a call to action
```

---

## Task 1.3 – Rewrite Content

Prompt:

```
Rewrite the LinkedIn post using a more exciting and conversational tone suitable for social media.
```

---

### Challenge

Ask Claude to generate:

- Blog introduction
- FAQ
- Product tagline
- Marketing slogan

# Exercise 3 – Report Generation

## Scenario

The sales team has provided the following weekly sales data.

| Region | Sales |
|----------|-------|
| North | $210,000 |
| South | $175,000 |
| East | $245,000 |
| West | $190,000 |

Customer satisfaction: **91%**

New customers: **420**

Product returns: **3.5%**

---

## Task 3.1 – Generate Executive Summary

Prompt:

```
Create an executive summary from the following business data.

Sales:

North: $210,000

South: $175,000

East: $245,000

West: $190,000

Customer Satisfaction: 91%

New Customers: 420

Returns: 3.5%

Include:

- Key achievements
- Major observations
- Areas for improvement
- Business recommendations
```

---

## Task 3.2 – Create Management Report

Prompt:

```
Convert the sales information into a professional management report.

Structure:

- Executive Summary
- Sales Performance
- Customer Metrics
- Risks
- Opportunities
- Recommendations
- Conclusion
```

---

## Task 3.3 – Improve the Report

Prompt:

```
Rewrite the report for presentation to senior executives.

Use concise language and executive-level insights.
```

---

### Challenge

Ask Claude to generate:

- Monthly report
- Quarterly report
- KPI dashboard summary
- Performance review report

# Exercise 4 – Business Analysis

## Scenario

A retail company has experienced declining sales during the last three months.

Monthly Sales

| Month | Revenue |
|---------|----------|
| January | $1.25M |
| February | $1.12M |
| March | $980K |

Additional Information

- Marketing spend decreased by 20%
- Customer complaints increased by 15%
- Competitor launched two new products
- Delivery delays increased

---

## Task 4.1 – Perform Business Analysis

Prompt:

```
Act as a Senior Business Consultant.

Analyze the following business data.

Sales dropped from $1.25M in January to $980K in March.

Additional factors:

- Marketing budget reduced by 20%
- Customer complaints increased by 15%
- Competitor launched two new products
- Delivery delays increased

Provide:

- Root cause analysis
- Key business risks
- Opportunities
- Recommendations
- Immediate action plan
```

---

## Task 4.2 – SWOT Analysis

Prompt:

```
Based on the business information, perform a SWOT analysis.

Present the output as a table.
```

---

## Task 4.3 – Executive Recommendations

Prompt:

```
If you were the CEO, what would be your top five priorities over the next 90 days?

Explain why each recommendation is important.
```

---

### Challenge

Ask Claude to:

- Create a risk assessment matrix
- Prioritize business initiatives
- Identify quick wins
- Suggest KPIs to monitor success
- Develop a 90-day improvement roadmap

---

# Key Takeaways

- Claude acts as a collaborative assistant, helping you draft, refine, and improve business documents.
- Clear prompts with context, audience, and desired format produce better results.
- Iterative prompting enables continuous improvement of outputs.
- Always review AI-generated content for accuracy, organizational policies, and tone before sharing.
